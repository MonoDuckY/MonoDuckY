### 🧭 About Me & Working Philosophy

Welcome to my cozy corner on GitHub! I am a Software Engineering student with a strong career orientation toward **Business Analysis (BA)** and **Project Management (PM)**. 

Having hands-on experience building full-stack web and mobile applications, I enjoy looking at software not just as lines of code, but as complete ecosystems—where clear documentation, structured workflows, and empathetic user experiences come together.

- 🔭 **What I do best:** Translating complex requirements into clear **SRS documents, UML diagrams (Use Case, Sequence, Class), WBS, and RACI matrices**, while collaborating closely with developers using Agile/Scrum practices.
- 💻 **Technical foundation:** Comfortable working across the stack (**Spring Boot, React/TypeScript, Flutter, Python**) to prototype ideas, understand system constraints, and communicate seamlessly with engineering teams.
- 🌱 **Currently learning:** Advancing my **Japanese for IT (ITSS / FE preparation)** and refining documentation-first product engineering.

### 🌟 Featured Showcase & Product Specification

#### ❄️ [Yoshino's Home (四糸乃の家) — Interactive Exhibition & Tribute Web App](https://github.com/MonoDuckY/Yoshino-home)
> *More than a fan-made tribute—this repository serves as my sandbox for **Documentation-First Engineering** and **Immersive UI/UX Design**.*

- **Product & UX Concept:** A horizontal-scrolling digital exhibition with a *"Warm Winter Daylight"* aesthetic, featuring an interactive guided tour and physical acrylic standee visual effects.
- **Documentation Highlight (BA/PM Focus):** Includes a comprehensive [**Design Specification (`DESIGN_SPEC.md`)**](https://github.com/MonoDuckY/Yoshino-home/blob/main/DESIGN_SPEC.md) and **Engineering Specification Hub** detailing system architecture, component flows, and content governance.
- **Tech Stack:** React 19, TypeScript 5.8, Vite 6, Tailwind CSS v4, Framer Motion, Sanity.io Headless CMS, and Vercel Serverless.
- 💬 **Say Hi:** Feel free to visit the [Live Web App](https://yoshino-home.vercel.app) and leave a note in the **Guestbook**!
